Explanation.html

The goal of the website is to provide information relating to retro video game-based emulation. This will include, but will not be limited to, popular emulators, safe methods of emulation on all platforms, and popular projects related to retro game console emulation. The intention being that any person who has used the website will leave with enough knowledge and experience to be able to emulate locally on their own devices, and understand how it should look when functioning properly.
